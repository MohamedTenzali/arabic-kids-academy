# Arabic Kids Academy Brand Guidelines

Arabic Kids Academy is a modern educational kids app for children aged 4-9. The brand should feel playful, warm, trustworthy, and premium without becoming busy or heavy.

## Personality

- Child friendly and encouraging
- Calm enough for parents to trust
- Playful enough for children to enjoy
- Clear, warm, and easy to scan

## Visual Direction

- Rounded UI with generous spacing
- Soft shadows and gentle gradients
- Large touch targets for children
- Minimal layouts with warm details
- Educational, not cluttered

## Core Palette

- Primary Purple: `--primary-purple`, `#6B3BF5`
- Growth Green: `--growth-green`, `#8BC34A`
- Playful Yellow: `--playful-yellow`, `#FFC83D`
- Friendly Pink: `--friendly-pink`, `#FF6FAE`
- Sky Blue: `--sky-blue`, `#4DB6FF`
- Soft Background: `--background-soft`, `#F8F7FF`
- Surface White: `--surface-white`, `#FFFFFF`
- Dark Text: `--text-dark`, `#2D2D2D`
- Light Text: `--text-light`, `#666666`

## Typography

- Headings: Baloo 2 or Fredoka
- Body: Poppins or Nunito

## UX Rules

- No sharp corners
- No dark heavy UI
- No visual clutter
- Consistent card style
- Consistent spacing
- Buttons must be large and easy to tap
- Soft modern gradients are allowed
