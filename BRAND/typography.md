## Rules

- Use rounded typography.
- Use large readable text.
- Do not use thin fonts.
- Do not use serif fonts.
- Use large spacing.
- Keep labels direct and encouraging.
- Use bold text for actions and progress.
- Do not use negative letter spacing.
